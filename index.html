Rubik's Cube World Record

The Rubik's Cube is a puzzle toy invented by Ernő Rubik in 1974. Over time, it has gained immense popularity and is now recognized as a mind sport, officially represented and governed by the WCA for competitions.

To this day, numerous Rubik's records have been broken—ranging from 2x2 to 7x7 cubes, as well as various side events—with records being updated after every competition on the official WCA website.
